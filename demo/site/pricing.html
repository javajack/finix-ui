Billing FAQ

The fine print, in plain words.

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — self-serve, effective at the end of the billing period, and we prorate annual plans on request. The MIT kit keeps working forever either way.
How does per-seat billing work?
A seat is anyone who signs into the Theme studio or private registry. Viewers are free and unlimited. Adding a seat mid-cycle is prorated automatically.
Do you offer invoicing?
Annual Pro plans over 10 seats and all Enterprise plans can pay by invoice (ACH/wire, Net 30).
Is there a discount for nonprofits or education?
Yes — 50% off Pro for registered nonprofits, and free Pro for classroom use. Write to us from your org email.
What happens to my themes if I downgrade?
Everything exports to plain CSS tokens — your themes are yours. Downgrading only pauses the studio tooling, never your shipped product.
