Surface hierarchy
Depth is encoded by lightness — every layer has a named token. Wells sit below surfaces; overlays float above them.
--background · canvas (dot-grid ground)
--card · surface (cards, tables, panels)
--well · inset (toolbars, column wells, code, headers)
--popover · overlay (menus, dialogs — lighter than cards in dark)

Plus --hover for every pointer-over fill and --border-soft for internal dividers — structural container borders always use --border.
